Show installment plans, upfront-payment discounts and accepted payment methods on your WooCommerce products.

== Description ==

WC Custom Installments answers the question every shopper asks before buying: *how much is it per month, and what do I save if I pay it all at once?*

It renders the installment plan under the product price, on the shop loop and on the product page, using the interest rate your acquirer actually charges. It shows the discounted price for upfront payment, carries that discount through the cart and checkout, and displays the card brands and payment methods you accept.

= Installments =

* Maximum installments, interest-free installments and a minimum installment amount
* A single monthly interest rate applied beyond the interest-free range
* The best installment shown under the price, with the full table in a popup or inline
* Installments displayed in the cart

= Discounts =

* A discount on the displayed price for upfront payment, as a percentage or a fixed amount
* The discount carried into the cart and checkout totals, with or without shipping
* Per-product opt-out for products that should not be discounted

= Payment methods =

* Pix, bank slip, credit and debit containers
* Bundled badges for the major card brands and payment providers

= Elsewhere on the site =

* Shortcodes for every element, usable in any page builder
* Elementor widgets for the price group and the installment table
* Compatibility with popular WooCommerce themes

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin to `/wp-content/plugins/`, or install it from Plugins > Add New.
2. Activate it.
3. The setup wizard opens automatically. You can reopen it later from WooCommerce > Custom Installments.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does this change the price customers are charged? =

The installment display does not. The upfront-payment discount does: it applies to the cart and checkout totals, which is the point of it.

= Does it work with variable products? =

Yes. The plan is calculated from the price of the selected variation.
